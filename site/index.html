Why a Cardominium

A shop that's actually yours.

Most of us wrench in a rented bay, a cramped two-car garage, or a driveway at 110 degrees. A Cardominium is a deeded garage condo built around the work: 20 foot ceilings for the lift, plumbing for the sink, and neighbors who speak fluent torque wrench.

The Build Sheet

Built to be built out.

Every bay comes ready for the work, and most owners will still put some effort into setting up their space. We handle the hard parts so you can build it your way.

  • Electrical service

    Service options to power welders, compressors and lifts. Available capacities to be confirmed.

  • 20' clearance

    20 foot ceilings, with room for a two-post lift or a mezzanine.

  • Mini split A/C

    Pre-plumbed lines for a mini split. Wrench in July without melting.

  • Half bath standard

    Sink and toilet hookups in every bay.

  • Floor drains

    Standard in every bay, so washdowns and spills are easy.

  • 24-hour access

    Gated and camera-monitored, with access any hour.
